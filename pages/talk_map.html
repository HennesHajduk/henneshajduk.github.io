Here you can see the locations, times, and occasions where I have given scientific presentations.

Each numbered circle is a cluster of nearby talks. Hover over one to see the area it covers, or click it to zoom in and split it into individual markers.

The talk-location data is scraped from this site's talk pages by a Jupyter Notebook, and the interactive map itself is generated with the Leaflet.markercluster plugin via the getorg library, following the approach from the GitHub AcademicPages Template. The basemap image is derived from ETOPO 2022 global relief data (NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information).
