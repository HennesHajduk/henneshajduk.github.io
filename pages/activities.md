## Research Visits

International research collaborations are an important part of my work. In addition to conference travel, I have conducted research visits at the following institutions:

- Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China
- Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL), California, USA (summer internship and subsequent research visits)
- Lappeenranta–Lahti University of Technology (LUT University), Finland
- Saarland University, Germany
- Scripps Institution of Oceanography, University of California San Diego, USA
- Technical University of Clausthal, Germany
- University of Bayreuth, Germany
- University of St. Andrews, United Kingdom
- University of Texas at Austin, USA (semester abroad)
- University of Zurich, Switzerland

These visits have fostered collaborations in computational mathematics, scientific computing, and geophysical fluid dynamics.

## Open-Source Software

Open-source scientific software plays a central role in my research. My group and I primarily use the C++ [MFEM](https://mfem.org) finite element library for the development and implementation of new numerical methods.

I have contributed to the MFEM ecosystem through the [Remhos](https://github.com/CEED/Remhos) miniapp developed within the CEED project. Previously, I have participated in the implementation of the [FESTUNG](https://github.com/FESTUNG) discontinuous Galerkin framework.

## Scientific Service

I have served as a reviewer for the following international journals:

- *Computers & Fluids*
- *Journal of Computational Physics*
- *SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing*

Furthermore, I co-organized the **MoST 2020** workshop on *Modeling and Simulation of Transport Phenomena*.

## Awards and Recognition

My work has been recognized through several awards, including the **Best Thesis Award** from the Department of Mathematics at TU Dortmund University and the **Ernst P. Stephan Award 2026 for Mathematics and the Environment**, awarded by the Foundation *Mathematics and the Environment*.

In addition, I have been nominated twice for the **MFEM Visualization Contest** awards.
